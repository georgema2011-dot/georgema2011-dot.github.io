Compact residential interior / 2024

Japandi Retreat

A smaller home, with room for everyday life.

A one-room apartment study exploring how a compact home can feel open and homely while supporting different activities. The L-shaped layout organises circulation, while partitions create privacy without fully enclosing each area. A raised tatami platform accommodates the fixed beds and an adjacent space for reading and studying, reducing the need for a separate desk.

Brief
Multifunctional living in a compact home
Approach
Circulation, partial separation and a tatami platform
Output
Residential layout proposal
