Conceptual industrial design / 2026

SOMEPEN

Four colours. One movement.

A conceptual proposal for a 4.5 cm finger-mounted pen for work that involves writing while moving. It explores four thumb-operated controls for common D1 refills and an elastomer ring that keeps the tool close between notes.
