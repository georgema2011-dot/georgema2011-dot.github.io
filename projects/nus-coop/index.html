NUS Co-op

Turning retail touchpoints into a campus service.

A three-person academic proposal to reconnect NUS Co-op with its community purpose. Student and frontline-staff journey mapping surfaced unclear stock information, cluttered navigation and operational overload. The proposal combines a simpler online experience, delivery or pickup choices, wider store circulation, expanded storage and flexible student-market fixtures.
