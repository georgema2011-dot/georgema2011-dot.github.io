Spatial computing + interaction / 2026

Visual Bridge: ACL Visualisation through the Visio Pro

Making knee anatomy easier to understand.

A spatial-computing education prototype for newly diagnosed ACL patients. The project explores layered knee anatomy and a spatial app flow to explain the injury and what comes next. Patient-specific damage and leg-linked movement are proposed next steps.
